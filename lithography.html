Positive Resist

  • Exposed areas become soluble
  • Light breaks polymer chains
  • Washed away in developer
  • Result mirrors mask clear regions
  • Used in most modern IC fabs

Negative Resist

  • Exposed areas become insoluble
  • Light cross-links polymer chains
  • Unexposed areas washed away
  • Result is inverted from mask
  • Better adhesion, lower resolution
